Hello everyone, this is SemiMinus.

I’m delighted that Shuumatsu Monji is my first public project on GitHub, even though it’s only had a few dozen views. <br>
However, I’ve decided to archive this repository. <br>
Although it’s 1 April where I am, this isn’t an April Fool’s joke.
<hr>
Here's the reason.<br>
- **I am simply too busy**. Although I would love to create glyphs for the majority of Chinese characters in this project, given that I am still a university student with very limited time each day, and there are approximately 3,500 commonly used characters, it is virtually impossible for me.<br>
- **Exhaustion**. I cannot bear repeating the same tasks day in, day out.<br>
- **Similar projects**. In fact, someone created a similar font quite some time ago. I don’t want to waste my time doing what others have already done. Besides, mine wouldn’t be any better than theirs.
<hr>
These are the reasons why I’ve decided to archive this repository.<br>
It’s a shame; I’d originally hoped to continue working on this font after the New Year, but I’ve now realised I have almost no time to make updates.<br>
I’ve also long since lost the motivation to continue. <br>
I’ve been so busy that I’ve only just found the time to write this. <br>
I’m very sorry. <br>
<hr>
Aside from my studies and font design, I’m also trying to find time to help a friend with the Chinese localisation of *The Tale of the Flower Pavilion*, and I’d like to translate a few articles as well. <br>
Perhaps I’ve been pushing myself too hard.
