# Shuumatsu Monji Project / “終末文字”项目

## What is "Shuumatsu Monji"? /什么是“終末文字”？
Shuumatsu Monji, or "終末文字" in Japanese Kanji, is a type of font used in Comic & TV Anime "Girls' Last Tour".<br>
[This](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Girls%27_Last_Tour) is the Wikipedia page of GLT, with some simple introduction.<br>
终末文字（しゅうまつもんじ）是漫画和TV动画“少女终末旅行”中出现的一种字体。<br>
以下链接包含对《少女终末旅行》的介绍。<br>
[维基百科](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%B0%91%E5%A5%B3%E7%B5%82%E6%9C%AB%E6%97%85%E8%A1%8C)  
[百度百科](https://baike.baidu.com/item/%E5%B0%91%E5%A5%B3%E7%BB%88%E6%9C%AB%E6%97%85%E8%A1%8C/21514450)

## Why do you make this repository? / 你为什么建了这个仓库？
This repository is for me to store the source of the font I (try to) make. After all, I feel not quite safe store those file in my PC. (Also it takes a lot of space of my disk)<br>
这个仓库会存储我（试图）做的字体的资源。因为我感觉只是把这些文件保存在我的电脑里不是很安全。（而且浪费我的电脑内存）<br>

## Why do you want to make this font? / 你为什么想做这个字体？
I'm a fan of GLT since 2019 when I watched the anime. I bought the Simplified Chinese translated version in 2025 (Although it's not important for the reason I had to say).<br>
When I surf in the Internet, I came across the Setting Set of the anime, and there's a picture attracted me.<br>
自从我2019年看了少末的动画后，我就很喜欢这部作品。（虽然我直到2025年才买了简中版漫画……也许是因为他们24年才出版？）<br>
我在网上找到了少末动画的设定集，其中一页吸引了我的注意。<br>

![少末设定集第80页](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d3d33931-428e-4c83-80f2-af0b46390c9c)

I'm a fan, or I don't know how I can call it, of design this kind of font myself. It occured to me that there isn't any GLT font made. So I decided to make my own.<br>
我很喜欢研究这种设计奇特的字体。但是我发现网上（至少是中文互联网，因为这就是我搜索的上限）没有做这种字体的。所以我想试着做一个。<br>

## How may you make this? / 你会怎么实现？
I decided to learn FontForge and Adobe Illustrator and try to achieve this. It's a long way to go, but I believe I can make it.<br>
我使用FontForge和Adobe Illustrator来制作这些字体。对我来说可能很难（我没有这些软件的使用经验——一点都没有！），但是我尽力。<br>

## Any other words? / 还有什么想说的吗？
I'm glad to do a project like this. Thanks to Tsukumizu(つくみず), the artist of the comic, and White Foxs, the producer of the anime.<br>
能够把这个项目作为我在GitHub上的第一个项目是很令人愉快的。<br>
感谢漫画作者つくみず和动画制作White Foxs让我接触到这部作品。<br>
